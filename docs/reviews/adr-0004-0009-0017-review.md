## 1. Overall Assessment

The decisions of all three **had long since existed in the §0 ledger and in the `AGENT.md` §2 non-goals**; this round is a backfill of the argumentation rather than a fresh adjudication. So the focus of the review is not "are the decisions right", but three things:

1. **Whether the basis went back to the original text.** The first Context paragraph of all three quotes the original text of the `AGENT.md` §2 non-goals directly, and the Evidence sections give the original quotations and file locations item by item. There was no case of "writing quotations from memory".
2. **Whether the accepted cost was written down.** All three have a Negative / accepted subsection, and what is listed is not a "to-do" but knowingly accepted gaps (0004 cannot satisfy play announcements by itself, 0009 drops calls on timeout, 0017 cannot provide billing data). This meets the discipline requirement of the ADR registry.
3. **Whether anything overstepped.** None of the three gives any capacity or timing conclusion: 0009 states explicitly that "the timeout value is an operational parameter; this ADR gives no capacity or timing conclusion", and the capacity drop during draining is described only qualitatively; 0017 states explicitly that O4 / D5 are undecided and does not hardcode a default value. This is consistent with `AGENT.md` §2 "publish no capacity numbers before the M6 measurements".

Structurally the three align with ADR-0002 / ADR-0006: four header lines (Status / Date / Decides / REQs addressed) → `---` → Context → Decision → Consequences (Positive / Negative-accepted) → Alternatives (table with Why not) → Evidence → Related.

## 3. Finding List and Adjudication

### 3.1 ADR-0004 (no media / media seam)

| # | Finding | Verdict | Adjudication and Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-0004-1 | The basis must be the original text of the `AGENT.md` §2 non-goals, not a summary such as "doing media off-premises is inappropriate" | Mandatory | Accept. The first Context paragraph quotes the non-goal original text verbatim (including the five negations), and the two reasons — triangular routing and compliance — are each developed and point back to the original text of architecture document §9.1 |
| F-0004-2 | If the two reasons (triangular routing and compliance) are written only as "detour + compliance", they degenerate into slogans | Should be developed | Accept. Each reason gets its own paragraph: triangular routing is split into bandwidth doubling and latency increase; compliance is placed alongside the LI non-goal in `AGENT.md` §2, explaining that "the location of the capability determines the location of the compliance responsibility" |
| F-0004-3 | If the seam only says "the interface is preserved", it becomes a loophole that can be exploited at any time | Mandatory | Accept. The third Decision defines the seam as two parts, "interface definition + trigger condition", limits the trigger conditions to four categories (play announcement / record / transcode / DTMF), and states that enabling it must re-run the ADR and requirement process (`AGENT.md` §3 documentation chain) |
| F-0004-4 | Known gap: play announcement / recording depends on an MRF inside the carrier network, and with no deliverable MRF currently available the requirement cannot be satisfied | Non-blocking | Accept and explicitly registered. The first Negative entry states that this is a **delivery boundary** that must be written into the interface agreement with the carrier; Consequences also registers the cost of "the seam sitting empty for a long time with no one daring to delete it". It also states truthfully that there is currently no media module under `platform/src`, without fabricating an existing interface implementation |

### 3.2 ADR-0009 (ISSU means draining)

| # | Finding | Verdict | Adjudication and Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-0009-1 | A common mis-statement: "the process is stateless, so it can be restarted freely". "Not migrating state" must be distinguished from "being allowed to kill the process immediately" | Mandatory | Accept. The second Context paragraph argues this distinction specifically and quotes ADR-0010's "there is no state in the process, which does not mean there are no in-flight calls in the process" |
| F-0009-2 | The mechanism must land in concrete files, not just "use preStop" | Mandatory | Accept. Decision ② splits it into two halves, the orchestration side (`preStop` + `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` in `deploy/helm/templates/deployment.yaml`) and the kernel side (`ProcessShell.request_terminate` / `run_until_drained`); Evidence gives the file, symbol and comment original text item by item |
| F-0009-3 | If a hard timeout is not given, the Pod will be held forever by calls that never end; if it is given, that amounts to admitting that calls will be dropped | Mandatory | Accept both. Decision ④ requires that there must be a hard timeout, while stating that "the timeout is an operational parameter; this ADR gives no capacity or timing conclusion"; the second Negative entry states that a timeout drops calls and that this **must be alerted on** rather than done silently |
| F-0009-4 | This decision rests on the premise that "the current stack state is not serializable", a premise that **will change**; writing it as an eternal conclusion is a latent hazard | Non-blocking, but must be registered | Accept. Decision ⑤ states that this decision may be re-evaluated once the E5 probe passes verification, and that overturning it requires a newly written ADR and must not be changed silently; Evidence quotes ADR-0019 §7 K6 and item 3 of the §7 accepted gaps (E5 unverified), and truthfully states that "E5 does not even have an executable verdict yet" |

### 3.3 ADR-0017 (no CDR)

| # | Finding | Verdict | Adjudication and Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-0017-1 | The basis must be the original text of the `AGENT.md` §2 non-goals and the PRD REQ-NF-7 original text | Mandatory | Accept. The first Context paragraph quotes the non-goal original text verbatim; Evidence quotes the full text of REQ-NF-7, including "a call trace is not a call detail record: it has no delivery channel, it is not rated, and it is not settled between networks" |
| F-0017-2 | It is easily misread as "keeping no evidence at all"; it must be explained that what is excluded is the form, not the requirement | Mandatory | Accept. The third Context paragraph distinguishes "no CDR" from "keeping no evidence", and quotes the PRD US-3 scenario to describe the querying party, the query key (Call-ID) and the purpose (complaint tracing / anti-fraud evidence) |
| F-0017-3 | If "a trace is not a call detail record" is written only in the document, downstream parties may still use it for reconciliation | Mandatory | Accept. Decision ③ requires that this distinction be **explicitly stated in both documentation and code**, and explains why (once it is used for reconciliation it has in fact become a second CDR, yet without delivery and settlement semantics); Evidence quotes the "**not a call detail record**" annotation in the architecture document §7 table |
| F-0017-4 | Known gap: retention period (O4) and storage selection (D5) are undecided | Non-blocking | Accept and explicitly registered. Decision ④ states that the retention period belongs to O4, is decided by customer compliance requirements, and that **no default value may be hardcoded before it is decided** (the mechanism lands first, the numbers do not); Negative entries two and three register O4 and D5 respectively, and entry four registers the storage pressure of "full volume per message, with no sampling-based reduction means" |
