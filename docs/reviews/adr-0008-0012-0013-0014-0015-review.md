## Review Method

| Dimension | Method |
|---|---|
| Source of basis | Clause-by-clause comparison against the original text of `AGENT.md` §2 (non-goals), §4 (layering), §5 (coding conventions), §6 (test strategy), §9 (gates), §11 (Git rules), §13 (security); the corresponding entries of the architecture document §0 ledger and the original text of §6.1 / §11.2 / §11.3 / §11.4; the original text of PRD v0.2 REQ-NF-1 / NF-3 / NF-9 / REQ-G-2 / G-3 / G-4 |
| Consistency with existing ADRs | Whether it conflicts with ADR-0002 (state externalization), 0003 (S-SBC transparent bridging), 0005 (OTel), 0007 (data plane), 0009 (ISSU draining), 0010 (HPA), 0016 (security), 0019 (stack selection, including K7) |
| Relationship to undecided items | Whether undecided items (O1 / O5 / D3 / D6) are wrongly treated as already adjudicated; whether unadjudicated items are explicitly marked |
| Truthfulness of landed evidence | Whether the referenced files exist and whether the quotations match the original text; whether unimplemented things are marked as gaps rather than fabricated |
| Structural completeness | Whether it has Context / Decision / Consequences (Positive + Negative-accepted) / Alternatives (including Why not) / Evidence / Related, and whether the four header fields are complete |

## Per-ADR Conclusions

### ADR-0008 (redundancy: zero single point of failure within a site; cross-site 1+1 warm standby, not active-active)

| # | Finding | Adjudication |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Both "zero single point of failure within a site" and "cross-site warm standby" quote the original text of architecture document §6.1 directly (including the three reasons for "not adopting active-active" and the N+M warning note), and are not rewritten from memory | Accept, as the L1 basis |
| 2 | It explicitly does not decide the undecided item O5: it establishes only the lower bound "zero single point of failure within a site", and leaves N+1 vs N+M to the customer's SLA | Accept; no overstepping adjudication, consistent with how ADR-0002 lists O5 as one of its own gaps |
| 3 | The third reason for not going active-active across sites lands on ADR-0002 (runtime state in Redis, cross-site replication conflicts with a single source of truth) rather than the vague "too complex" | Accept; the reason is traceable to a specific ADR |
| 4 | No cross-site RTO / RPO numbers are given at all | Accept as a **known gap**, non-blocking; it is already stated in Consequences that these cannot be given before O5 and the customer SLA are adjudicated |
| 5 | The assignment of failover drills to M5 / M8 is an inference from the milestone table, not a direct statement in the original text | Accept as a **reasonable inference** and already marked as "evidence gathering falls into these two places"; it is recommended that the M8 acceptance items explicitly include a "cross-site failover drill" — a follow-up action, non-blocking for this ADR |
