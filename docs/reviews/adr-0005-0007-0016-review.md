## Per-ADR Conclusions

### ADR-0005 (OTel three signals)
| # | Finding | Adjudication |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quotes the original text of `AGENT.md` §5 and §13 as evidence rather than relying on memory | Accept, as the L1 basis |
| 2 | Clearly distinguishes "call trace (business query channel, replacing CDR)" from "trace (troubleshooting telemetry)", and explains why they do not share storage | Accept; this distinction removes the conceptual confusion with REQ-NF-7 / ADR-0017 |
| 3 | Drops events once the bounded queue is full, and exposes the loss via a `dropped` counter | Accept; the "telemetry loss acceptable, backpressure on the call path unacceptable" trade-off matches its consequences |
| 4 | Metric names and labels are not finalized, and REQ-NF-14 (alert rule set) depends on them | Accept as a **known gap**, non-blocking for this ADR; already listed as an M5 prerequisite |
